Something Fresh is
Coming Downstairs

Grand Opening: July 13th

Brought to you by the team behind Estudillo Produce & Deli, we are thrilled to introduce San Leandro’s newest neighborhood spot: The Downstairs Counter.

Born from our passion for high-quality ingredients and community, we are creating a welcoming space right here in the neighborhood. We will be serving fresh, high-quality coffee, teas, matcha, a curated selection of teas, artisanal sandwiches, fine cheeses, delicious pastries and snacks. To perfectly complement your day, we also offer a fine selection of wines alongside refreshing sparkling drinks.

We are polishing the final details and cannot wait to welcome you soon. See you Downstairs!

Community & Purpose

Proud Partners of Kids & Art

At The Downstairs Counter, we believe in feeding both our community and its spirit. That’s why we are proud to partner with Kids & Art, a California-based nonprofit that provides free creative art experiences for children touched by cancer and serious medical conditions, along with their families.

✨ Visit our gallery downstairs: We are honored to display beautiful artwork created by these incredibly talented children right on our walls. Come grab a coffee and take a look at their inspiring creations in person!

"Creating opportunities for comfort, connection, and creativity when they are needed most."
